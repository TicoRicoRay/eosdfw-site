Privacy Policy.

Short version: this is a small business website. It uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are popular, but it doesn’t sell your data, run ad trackers, or drop marketing cookies. Here’s the honest breakdown of what happens when you visit.

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Who runs this site

This website (eosdfw.com) is operated by Ray Myers, an independent Professional EOS Implementer® based in Plano, Texas, serving clients in the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex and nationwide. Contact: {{ site.primary_email }}.

What personal information is collected on this site

Directly by this website: none of the identifying kind. There are no contact forms, no newsletter signups, and no accounts to create. The only automated data collection is aggregate, non-advertising analytics (see the next section), and no marketing or advertising pixels are loaded.

Personal information is collected only when you choose to reach out, through one of three channels:

  • Email. If you email {{ site.primary_email }}, your name, email address, and whatever you write are received in Ray’s email inbox and processed under the privacy policy of the email provider (Microsoft / Outlook).
  • Calendly. If you click “Book a call” or “Schedule on my calendar,” you are handed off to Calendly, which collects the information required to schedule a meeting (name, email, time zone, any notes you add). That data is handled under Calendly’s privacy policy.
  • Phone or in-person conversation. If you call or meet with Ray, notes may be kept in a private CRM or notebook for the purpose of following up on the conversation.

Any information you share this way is used only to respond to you, schedule a meeting, or continue an ongoing client relationship. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to profile you.

What is collected automatically

Google Analytics 4. This site uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to measure aggregate visitor traffic: which pages are viewed, what device and browser is used, approximate geographic region, and referral source. GA4 is configured on this site with the following privacy-protective settings:

  • IP anonymization is enabled. Full IP addresses are not stored or reported.
  • Google Signals is disabled. No cross-device tracking, no demographics or interest reports, and no linking of your visits to a Google advertising profile.
  • Ad personalization signals are disabled. Data collected here is not shared with Google Ads or used to build remarketing audiences.
  • Event data retention is set to 2 months (the shortest GA4 permits), with user data reset on new activity.

GA4 sets first-party cookies (typically named _ga and _ga_<stream_id>) to distinguish unique visitors and sessions. These cookies contain a randomly generated identifier, not your name or email. You can opt out browser-wide by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking third-party scripts in your browser settings.

Hosting and delivery logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Both providers automatically log basic technical information for every visitor to any website they serve (IP address, browser type, timestamp, requested URL) for security, abuse prevention, and reliability. Ray does not receive, review, or retain these server logs.

Google Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches a font file, Google receives your IP address as part of the standard web request. Google Fonts does not set cookies for this purpose.

Beyond the GA4 cookies described above, no other first-party cookies are set by this site. No advertising or social-media pixels are loaded.

Third parties linked from this site

External links (Calendly, LinkedIn, YouTube, EOS Worldwide, embedded YouTube videos, and any sources cited in blog posts) are governed by the privacy policies of those services. Embedded YouTube videos may set cookies once you press play.

Your California privacy rights (CCPA / CPRA)

The California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the CPRA, gives California residents specific rights over their personal information, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

Ray Myers, EOS Implementer, is a small independent practice and does not meet the thresholds that make a business subject to the CCPA (see the California Privacy Protection Agency FAQ). Nevertheless, as a matter of policy:

  • We do not sell your personal information and never have.
  • We do not share your personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • If you have contacted Ray by email or booked a meeting and would like the associated records deleted, email {{ site.primary_email }} with the subject line “Privacy request” and we will honor the request within a reasonable time.

Your rights under other laws

If you are located outside the United States (for example, in the EU/UK under GDPR/UK GDPR), the same practical position applies: no marketing tracking, no data sale, and any information you volunteer is used only to respond to you. Contact {{ site.primary_email }} to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you’ve shared.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

This site does not currently detect or act on browser “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signals in a technical way, because the industry has not converged on a standard for how GA4 should honor them. If you want to opt out of Google Analytics on this site, use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, block third-party scripts in your browser, or use a privacy-focused browser or extension (such as uBlock Origin or Brave) that blocks GA4 by default.

Children

This site is intended for business owners and leadership teams. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
